# The Legacy of Goku 4 — Dragon Ball GT, AF & God of Destruction DLC

## Play
1. mGBA → `ROM/LegacyOfGoku4_GT_DLC.gba` (16 MB)
2. New Game → reach **Snake Way** (Other World)
3. Talk to **East Kai** (extra NPC — King Kai still there) to unlock **SSJ4** (red fur, separate from SS3).
4. **Whis's Portal & Beerus's Planet**: Speak with **Whis** to visit **Beerus's Planet** in the Other World, buy gourmet items at Whis's Shop (*Beerus's Pudding*, *Tree of Might Fruit*), and challenge **Beerus (Bills)** (`150,000 HP`, Level 250).
5. **Post-Game Boss Zaiko**: After defeating **Kid Buu**, travel to **East District 439 Forest** (outside **Adult Gohan's house**) to battle **Zaiko / Xicor** (`99,999 HP`, Level 200).
6. **Ultimate Fusion — Gogeta SSJ4**: Unlock Form ID `0x1B` + **Big Bang Kamehameha x100** after defeating Zaiko and Beerus.
7. SS3 remains fully original.

## Form ladder
**Gogeta SSJ4 (form 0x1B) > SS4 (form 1) > SS3 (form 5, original) > SS1 (form 3) > Base (form 0)**
